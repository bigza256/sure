About SURE

Track the Pool. Follow Every Bet.

Our mission

Radical transparency in pooled betting

SURE is a pooled-betting tracking and account-management platform. Our job is simple: make every contribution, every bet, and every distribution visible and auditable. We don't promise returns. We don't sell dreams. We record what actually happened — pending bets, published odds, realized results, and the reserve committed to ongoing activity — so contributors can see exactly where the pool stands at any moment.

🔍

Transparency

Every ledger entry is recorded. Balances, odds, and results are shown as entered — never fabricated or adjusted for display.

🔐

Accountability

All balance-changing operations produce a matching transaction record. Nothing is overwritten without a corresponding ledger entry.

📱

Accessibility

Built mobile-first for Android devices, so contributors across Uganda can review their account and the pool from any phone.

What we do

Track contributionsEvery contribution recorded with its verification status
Publish betting activityPending and completed bets with actual odds, markets, and stakes
Maintain a ledgerAppend-only transaction records for every balance change
Separate balancesAvailable balance vs. reserve / committed funds, clearly distinguished
Run 30-day cyclesCycle-by-cycle accounting based on actual realized results

What we do not do

  • We do not guarantee profits or any fixed return.
  • We do not present hypothetical returns as if they were achieved.
  • We do not use real customer testimonials, profits, or betting histories in this demo.
  • We do not approve deposits automatically — every submission is reviewed manually.
  • We do not claim a licence or regulatory status unless verified information is supplied by the administrator.

Terminology

In this initial version, SURE uses the terms pool participants and contributors rather than "shareholders", because the legal structure does not currently support shareholder terminology.

⚠️
Risk acknowledgement: Sports betting involves significant risk. Past results do not guarantee future results. Contributors should only commit money they can afford to lose.

This is a demonstration build. All balances, bets, and results shown are simulated. Production deployment requires hardened Firebase Security Rules, server-side validation, and appropriate regulatory review.
