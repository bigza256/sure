How SURE Works

From contribution to realized results — a clear, step-by-step explanation.

1

Register & contribute

Create your account, then submit a contribution between UGX 50,000 and UGX 800,000 with the Mobile Money transaction reference and a screenshot. Deposits are manually reviewed — screenshots do not auto-approve them.

2

Your balance splits into two parts

Once approved, your contribution becomes available balance (withdrawable under the rules) or reserve / committed balance (funds committed to pool betting activity, released only after settlement).

3

Follow the pool

The pool page shows total contributions, committed amounts, realized profit, realized losses, and each participant's contribution percentage — calculated transparently from recorded contributions.

4

Track every bet

Pending bets show the match, competition, market, planned stake, and the odds actually entered by the admin. When a bet closes, it moves to completed bets with the realized result: WIN, LOSS, or VOID.

5

Realized results drive distributions

Profit distributions are only recorded when there is actual realized profit from a bet. There are no promised daily returns, no guaranteed final amounts, and no income projections.

6

30-day cycle settlement

Each cycle runs for 30 days. At the end of the cycle, settlement is calculated from actual pool performance and the published terms — never from a projected amount.
