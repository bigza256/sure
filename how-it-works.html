How SURE Works

From contribution to realized results — a clear, step-by-step explanation.

1

Register & submit a contribution

Create your account, then submit a contribution between UGX 50,000 and UGX 800,000. Send the payment to the designated Mobile Money number shown on the deposit page and submit the transaction reference. Deposits are manually reviewed by an administrator — screenshots do not auto-approve them.

2

Your contribution splits into three wallets

Once approved, your contribution is divided: Active Allocation (the portion allocated to pool activity), Protected Reserve (held under the cycle rules), and Available Earnings (grows from realized results and is the only wallet eligible for withdrawal).

3

Follow the pool

The pool page shows total contributions, active allocations, protected reserves, available earnings, and total company share — all calculated from actual recorded activity. No projections, no estimated profits.

4

Track every bet

Pending bets show the match, competition, market, planned stake, and the odds actually entered by the admin. When a bet closes, it moves to completed bets with the realized result: WIN, LOSS, or VOID.

5

Realized results drive distributions

When an eligible allocation wins, gross profit is distributed across three destinations per the platform's rules: user available earnings, protected reserve addition, and company share. Losing stakes are recorded transparently.

6

30-day cycle settlement

Each cycle runs for 30 days. At the end of the cycle, settlement is calculated from actual pool performance — never from a projected amount. Your final position shows exactly where every part of your contribution and earnings went.
