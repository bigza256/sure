3.Account registration

You agree to provide accurate information (full name, phone number, email) and to keep it up to date. You are responsible for the security of your login credentials. One account per person, unless otherwise approved in writing.

4.Contributions

Contributions must be between UGX 50,000 and UGX 800,000 per transaction. Contributions are recorded only after manual verification by the administrator. Uploading a screenshot does not auto-approve a deposit. Only an approved deposit updates your balance and creates a ledger entry.

5.Pool operation

Contributions form a pool. Each participant's contribution percentage is calculated as their total contributions divided by the total pool contributions. Percentages are displayed transparently and may change as new contributions are approved. The pool is not a company, a shareholding structure, or a collective investment scheme unless such a structure is separately and explicitly established.

6.Betting activity

The administrator publishes betting activity. Bets are placed by the pool, not by individual participants. Pending bets, actual stakes, actual odds, and completed results are recorded and displayed on the platform.

7.Actual odds and results

Odds displayed on the platform are the odds actually entered by the administrator for each bet. SURE does not alter, smooth, or fabricate odds or results. If a bet's odds are corrected by the administrator, the change is logged with the previous value, the new value, the admin ID, and a timestamp.

8.Winning days

A winning day is a day on which the pool records a net realized profit from the day's bets. Winning days do not guarantee future winning days. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

9.Losing days

A losing day is a day on which the pool records a net realized loss. Losing days reduce the reserve. SURE does not guarantee that any day will be a winning day, and losing streaks can occur. Losses are shown transparently and are not hidden or smoothed.

10.Reserve / committed funds

Reserve funds are committed to ongoing betting activity. They are not immediately withdrawable. Release of reserve funds is subject to cycle settlement based on actual realized results and these Terms.

11.Profit distributions

Distributions are recorded only when actual realized profit exists and the pool's accounting rules permit a distribution. Distributions are allocated to participants in proportion to their contribution percentage. There is no promised distribution, no fixed distribution rate, and no guaranteed income.

12.Monthly settlement

Each cycle runs for 30 days. At the end of a cycle, settlement is calculated from the pool's actual performance and these Terms. There is no guaranteed final amount. Any figure displayed for illustration is not a promise of settlement.

13.Withdrawals

Withdrawals can only be requested against available balance (not reserve). Withdrawal requests are processed manually by the administrator and move through the statuses PENDING → PROCESSING → COMPLETED, or may be REJECTED. SURE does not claim that funds have been sent until a withdrawal is marked COMPLETED by the administrator and reflected in the ledger.

14.Failed or reversed transactions
