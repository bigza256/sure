Contribution Bands

SURE does not sell "packages" or promise fixed returns. Below are the contribution bands and what they mean in the pool's accounting.

⚠️
Important: Contribution bands describe how much you can contribute in a single transaction. They do not imply a fixed daily, weekly, or monthly return. Distributions depend entirely on actual realized betting results and the terms.
Entry

UGX 50,000 – 199,000

How a contribution percentage is calculated

Contribution percentage = your total contributions ÷ total pool contributions.

What is not in a plan

  • No guaranteed daily income.
  • No promised monthly return.
  • No fixed final payout amount.
  • No "risk-free" tier.
  • No automatic profits merely from holding a contribution.
