Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about contributions, bets, and distributions.

What is SURE?

SURE is a pooled-betting tracking and account-management platform. It records contributions, publishes betting activity with actual odds, tracks pending and completed bets, and maintains a transparent ledger for every balance change. SURE is not a bookmaker and does not place bets on users' behalf — it provides tracking and accounting around the pool's betting activity.

What is the minimum contribution?

UGX 50,000 per contribution.

What is the maximum contribution?

UGX 800,000 per contribution.

How are bets selected?

Bets are selected and published by the pool administrator. Each pending bet is shown with the match, competition, market, planned stake, and the odds actually entered by the admin.

Can I see pending bets?

Yes. Every pending bet is visible on the Bets page and on the home page. Pending bets show the match, market, planned stake, and the odds as entered by the admin.

Can I see completed bets?

Yes. Completed bets show the match, actual odds, actual stake, the result (WIN, LOSS, or VOID), the realized profit or loss, the date closed, and the bet reference where available.

What happens when a bet loses?

The stake is lost. No distribution is recorded for a losing bet. The pool's realized losses are recorded against the applicable cycle, and the reserve for that activity decreases. Losses are shown transparently on the Pool page.

What is the reserve?

The reserve is the portion of contributions that is currently committed to pool betting activity. Reserve funds are not immediately withdrawable — they are only released after a cycle settles according to actual realized results and the published terms.

When can I withdraw?

Withdrawals can be requested against your available balance (not the reserve). Every withdrawal request is processed manually by the administrator. There is no automatic payout, and SURE does not claim money has been sent until a withdrawal is marked completed by the admin.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. SURE does not guarantee profits. Distributions depend entirely on actual realized betting results and the applicable terms. Sports betting involves significant risk — you can lose some or all of the funds exposed to betting.

How are deposits verified?

Deposits are verified manually by the administrator. You submit the amount, the Mobile Money transaction reference, the date and time of payment, and optionally a screenshot. A screenshot alone does not auto-approve a deposit. Only an admin approval updates your balance and creates a ledger entry.

How are withdrawals processed?

You submit a withdrawal request with the amount, Mobile Money number, and account name. The administrator reviews it manually and moves it through the statuses PENDING → PROCESSING → COMPLETED (or REJECTED). Only when marked COMPLETED is the amount debited and recorded in your ledger.

How does the monthly cycle work?

Each cycle runs for 30 days. During the cycle, winning days and losing days are recorded from actual bet results. At the end of the cycle, settlement is calculated from the pool's actual performance and the published terms — never from a projected or promised amount.

What happens during a losing streak?

Losing streaks can occur. During a losing streak, realized losses accumulate, the reserve for betting activity decreases, and distributions are not recorded unless the pool's accounting rules allow them from actual realized profit. SURE does not promise daily income and does not smooth results to hide losing days.

Do you use real customer testimonials or profits?

No. This build does not present real testimonials, real customer numbers, or real profits. All figures shown in the demonstration are simulated and clearly labelled as such.

Is SURE licensed or regulated?

This build does not claim any licence or regulatory status. Any claim of licensing or regulation will only be made if the administrator supplies verified licence information. Users should review the Terms and Risk Disclosure before contributing.
