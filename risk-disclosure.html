Please read this before contributing to the pool.

Sports betting can result in losses

No bet is guaranteed to win. Every stake carries the risk of complete loss.

🎲

Odds are not guarantees

An odd of 1.80 means the market implies an outcome, not that the outcome will happen.

📊

Winning history doesn't predict future wins

A strong run of results does not improve the probability of the next bet.

🔻

Losing streaks can occur

Multiple losing bets in a row are normal and can substantially reduce the pool.

💧

The reserve can decrease

The reserve reflects committed funds. It is not a fixed amount and can shrink with losses.

💸

You can lose part or all of exposed funds

Funds committed to betting activity are at risk of partial or total loss.

📋

Distributions depend on results

No distribution is recorded unless actual realized profit exists under the pool's rules.

🚫

Do not contribute what you cannot lose

Only commit funds you are prepared to lose entirely.
